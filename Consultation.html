How it works

From intake to care plan in three steps.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which states do you serve?

HRT.org providers are currently licensed in Florida and California. If you live elsewhere, join the waitlist as we expand.

How long until I see a provider?

Most intakes are reviewed within 24 hours. Video visits are typically scheduled within 2–3 business days.

Do I need lab work before my consultation?

Not required to begin. Your provider will review any existing labs and recommend further testing if needed. We offer at-home kits for hormone, thyroid, and adrenal panels.

Can I use insurance?

We don't bill insurance directly, but visits are HSA/FSA eligible. You can submit receipts for out-of-network reimbursement.

What if I need to cancel or get a refund?

Consultations are refundable before the provider begins their review. See our Return Policy for details.
