# HRT chatbot audit and implemented improvements

Reviewed 26 October 2026. Changes apply to the local chatbot at `F:/HRT Chatbot`. The website widget's HTML, CSS, images and JavaScript are unchanged by this audit. No public deployment, account login, purchase, appointment booking or patient-data access was performed.

## Findings and causes

- A failed October 19 refresh retained the old product catalog but replaced knowledge sources with a partial crawl. Four reviewed entries (support, shipping restrictions, collection and results) disappeared from active retrieval. Only 12 of 16 reviewed entries were active.
- Whole-document hashes invalidated useful facts after unrelated page/navigation changes. Rejections were silent. Webflow leaf-div text, short headings and some contact/navigation links were missed by extraction.
- Substring keyword matching returned unrelated service content for login and insurance questions. Product search discarded vitamin letters and mishandled natural price questions. Men's generic summaries did not adequately answer women's service details.
- The actual portal destination was stored in an action field, but chat citations linked to the homepage. Follow-ups retained prose without adequately revalidating cited source IDs.
- The lowest product price could refer to a sold-out variant while another variant made the product appear available. Unknown current prices must remain unknown.
- Model prose was sent before final schema/source validation. An early test of a legitimate payment-capability refusal was rejected by overly narrow validation. Generic descriptions of other profiles initially confused a cortisol-test follow-up.

## Implemented changes

- Refreshed and reconciled all 1,177 public products, 1,576 variants and 46 collections. Twelve product-detail samples were checked through both JSON and availability endpoints. No added/removed products or product-level price/stock differences were found against the preceding catalog snapshot.
- Expanded active reviewed knowledge to 37 entries, backed by 38 normalized source documents. Includes care pathways, distinct patient/store accounts, providers, service details for men and women, eligibility, insurance, appointment changes, pharmacy information, testing, shipping, returns and privacy distinctions.
- Every reviewed entry retains source URL, fetch/review dates and full evidence anchors. Conflicting cross-page facts retain corroborating evidence. Facts are validated against complete evidence blocks and published action links; changed evidence is rejected with a diagnostic reason. Raw crawl text and marketing evidence anchors are not passed to the model as approved claims.
- Failed refreshes retain previous documents with their original dates and an explicit unverified flag. Unchanged facts survive irrelevant layout additions. Future crawls include previously known official pages. Manual review validation no longer overwrites the richer corpus with a hardcoded seed list.
- Added exact-word, topic-aware knowledge ranking and service aliases; preserved vitamin A/C/D and other meaningful terms. Product prices are tied to available variants, with unknown prices preserved. Visible product cards still receive live price/stock rechecks.
- Revalidate previous sources and displayed products on follow-ups. Render verified portal/action links. Buffer prose until schema/citation validation, retry malformed/ungrounded output once, and give an honest unavailable response if verification cannot finish.
- Refined instructions for concise direct answers, one useful clarification, source conflicts/freshness, exact product versus generic profile specifications, no invented policies/prices/benefits, and clinician boundaries. A zero-priced sold-out therapy listing is not treated as free care. Portal navigation is not blocked behind a treatment-geography question. Emergency routing clears shopping context and distinguishes plainly educational wording from current personal symptoms.

## Sources and known gaps

- Direct crawl: 40 business/account URLs, 39 HTTP 200 and one HTTP 404. `www.hrt.org` correctly redirects to `hrt.org`. A later normalization pass fetched 38 source documents successfully.
- Catalog crawl: 136 successful requests covering paginated products, two product sitemaps, all 46 collections and 12 detail samples. This does not mean every product's HTML detail page was independently visited.
- Final citation/product-link sweep: 33 public URLs, 32 HTTP 200; the only 404 is the known footer link `https://hrt.org/health-information-privacy-policy/`, not a returned answer source. The working notice is `https://hrt.org/legal/health-information-privacy-practices`.
- Insurance: main terms describe cash-pay/no insurance billing/no superbills, while store FAQs describe partner-lab billing and reimbursement. Confirm the exact service/test with HRT and the payer; no coverage promise is made.
- Consultation/package prices, inclusion of consultations, refund windows/sale exclusions and result-turnaround ranges conflict across official pages. These are explicitly flagged rather than reconciled by assumption.
- The standalone store shipping-policy page lacks substantive policy text. The main contact page contains a suspect Australia template address. Neither is treated as verified shipping/clinic guidance.
- Portal URLs return JavaScript application shells. Authenticated screens, password recovery, private results, live appointments and order status were not tested. Image-only supplement labels were not transcribed. Marketing claims were not independently clinically validated.

## Verification

- Baseline automated tests: 33/35 passed; two exposed incomplete catalog-report/knowledge data.
- Final automated tests: 63/63 passed, covering retrieval relevance, source retention/freshness, changed-evidence rejection, extraction, variant price/stock consistency, source-ID/schema validation, hostile instructions, session/API boundaries, and urgent versus educational wording.
- First live HTTP/SSE evaluation: 24 questions across 20 scenarios; 23 answers and one rejected payment-capability refusal. That defect was corrected and retested. A product-profile ambiguity was also corrected and retested.
- Revised live HTTP/SSE evaluation: 24/24 questions returned answers without provider/schema errors. Responses and citations were inspected for support, services, consultation/pricing, insurance, shipping, results, refunds, product facts/budgets, follow-ups, ordering limits, private order requests, ambiguous discovery, unpublished policy, medication dosing, pregnancy, emergency symptoms and prompt injection. This is representative testing, not proof that every possible answer will be correct.
- Actual browser widget verification and post-apply file checks are recorded in the workspace audit report.

## Maintenance

The local launcher currently disables scheduled refreshes. Use the protected refresh endpoint or the existing refresh command deliberately, then run `node scripts/review-knowledge.js` and inspect rejected entries in the admin report. Freshness is based on each source's last successful fetch, not the overall job date. Changed evidence requires editorial review; never automatically approve new medical or policy claims. Preserve known conflicts until HRT resolves them in its published sources. The project still uses bounded lexical retrieval, not an embedding/vector database.

The workspace `.chatbot-audit` retains dated crawl logs, source/link verification, initial and revised synthetic live evaluations, test output, applied-file hashes and backups. Runtime provenance is in `data/source-audit.json`, `data/knowledge-sources.json`, `data/knowledge-review.json` and `data/import-report.json`.
