About You

What's your age?

GLP-1 therapy is for adults. We'll ask a few short questions to see if you qualify — this takes about 2 minutes.

Measurements

Your height and weight.

BMI is one qualifying factor. ≥30 generally qualifies; 27–29.9 qualifies with a comorbidity like type 2 diabetes or hypertension.

Safety Screening

Are you currently pregnant, trying to conceive, or breastfeeding?

GLP-1 therapy is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Location

HRT.org providers are currently licensed in Florida and California. We're expanding — we'll let you know when you can be seen.
