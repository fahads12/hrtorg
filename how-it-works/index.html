The full journey

What to expect at every step.

A closer look at each stage of your care. Specific services, lab panels, and timing depend on your evaluation.

1Explore your concerns

Notice what has changed.

Think about your symptoms, your goals, and the questions you want to ask. Our free symptom assessments help you organize what you're feeling before you book — hormone health, weight, sexual health, hair, energy, sleep, mood.

The assessments are a starting point. They do not diagnose; they help you walk into a visit prepared.

Browse symptom guides →

2Complete your online visit

Share your health picture.

A secure intake collects your medical history, current medications and supplements, allergies, and any lab reports you have on hand. Everything is reviewed by a licensed clinician — not a bot.

Your information is encrypted end-to-end and shared only with your care team. We never sell your data.

3Lab testing when it matters

The right test for your question.

Hormone care often starts with labs. Your clinician may order a panel to measure testosterone, estrogen, thyroid, cortisol, or metabolic markers — collected at home with a finger-stick kit or at a nearby lab, whichever suits you.

Results come back in a few business days and get folded into your care plan.

See lab panels →

4Review and plan together

A real conversation about what works for you.

Your clinician discusses your symptoms, history, and any labs — then recommends a plan: specific therapy, dose, delivery method, and expected timeline. You get to ask questions, weigh alternatives, and understand the risks before committing.

A visit never guarantees a prescription. Treatment is recommended only when it's clinically appropriate for you.

5Care that keeps up

Delivery, follow-up, adjustments.

If medication is prescribed, it ships in discreet packaging from a licensed pharmacy. Your care team checks in on how you're feeling, adjusts your plan as your body responds, and sends refills on time — no new appointment needed for routine renewals.

Message your clinician any time through the portal. Follow-up labs are built into the plan where they add value.

Common questions

Know what to expect.

If you have a question that isn't here, our team is a message away.

Contact HRT.org →
How long does the first visit take?

The online intake takes about 10 minutes. After you submit, a clinician typically reviews it within 1–3 business days and reaches out through the portal with next steps — whether that's a live video visit, a lab order, or a plan to discuss.

Do I need lab testing before I can be prescribed anything?

For hormone therapies, yes — most of the time. Baseline labs help your clinician choose the right therapy and dose for you, and they give you a point of comparison when it's time to adjust. Some non-hormonal services (certain weight-management or sexual-health treatments, for example) may not require labs upfront.

Where is HRT.org available?

Our clinicians are licensed in all 50 states. Specific treatments and lab-collection options vary by state based on local regulations. During intake we confirm what's available where you live.

Does a visit guarantee I'll get a prescription?

No. Your clinician recommends treatment only when it's clinically appropriate. If a prescription isn't right for you — or if additional evaluation is needed first — they'll walk you through the alternatives and the reasoning.

How much does it cost?

Membership covers the clinical visit, messaging, and plan management. Lab panels and medications are billed separately at transparent prices — you see the cost before you authorize anything. We don't bill insurance directly; HRT.org can provide superbills on request for you to submit for reimbursement.

How will my prescription arrive?

If medication is prescribed, it ships in discreet packaging from a licensed U.S. pharmacy, typically within 3–5 business days of your plan being approved. Refills are handled automatically on your schedule.

Can I switch or stop treatment later?

Yes. You can message your clinician any time to adjust your dose, change therapy, pause, or stop. There are no long-term contracts.

Is my information private?

Yes. Visits, messages, and records are HIPAA-protected and encrypted end-to-end. Your information is shared only with your care team and the licensed pharmacy filling your prescription. We never sell your data.

What if I already have a provider?

HRT.org can work alongside your existing care. You can share records with your other providers, and your HRT.org clinician can coordinate on labs or treatment where it makes sense. Just mention this during intake.

Where do I sign in once I'm a patient?

Through the patient portal. You'll find your care plan, messages, lab results, upcoming visits, and refill status all in one place.
