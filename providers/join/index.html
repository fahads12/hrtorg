FAQ

Common questions

Do you verify provider credentials?
Yes. Every listing is cross-checked against the NPI registry and state licensure boards before going live. We also run a brief video verification call during onboarding.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Monthly memberships cancel at the end of the current billing cycle with no fees. Your Basic (free) listing stays live.
How do featured slots work?
Each city page has a defined set of featured slots. First come, first served — the first provider to subscribe in a city locks that slot. Premium tier is exclusive: only one Premium provider per city.
Do you take a cut of patient revenue?
No. HRT.org charges a flat monthly listing fee. We don't take percentages, referral fees, or billing data. The patient relationship is yours.
What if HRT.org also offers telehealth in my city?
HRT.org currently sees patients in California and Florida via telehealth and is expanding state by state. In your market, we present featured local providers alongside the HRT.org telehealth option when it's active — patients who prefer in-person care route to you.
