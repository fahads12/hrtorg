FAQ

Oakland HRT questions

Can I get HRT in Oakland without an in-person visit?
Oakland patients work with HRT.org clinicians licensed in California. Visits happen over secure video, labs ship directly to your Oakland address, and prescriptions route to your preferred pharmacy — including local independents and major chains in the Oakland area.
How much does HRT cost in Oakland?
HRT.org consultations start at $149 and medications typically run $35–$150 per month depending on protocol. Pricing is the same across every city — HSA and FSA accepted.
Do I need a referral or existing labs?
No referral. Start with the HRT.org symptom tracker, and your provider will order the appropriate labs. Recent lab results from a local Oakland provider are also accepted.
Is bioidentical HRT available?
Yes. HRT.org prescribes FDA-approved bioidentical products (estradiol patches, oral progesterone, testosterone cream) and compounded preparations where a specific dose or combination isn't available commercially.
