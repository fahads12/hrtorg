How to use

Using Sermorelin

Using our Sermorelin peptide injections is simple and can be done at home. After your initial consultation and prescription, you'll receive detailed instructions on how to properly store, prepare, and inject the peptide. Typically administered subcutaneously (under the skin), Sermorelin is taken once daily, usually at night to align with your body’s natural growth hormone cycle. Always follow the dosage and schedule recommended by your healthcare provider for best results. Side effects of Sermorelin Side effects of Sermorelin are generally mild and well-tolerated. Some users may experience redness, swelling, or discomfort at the injection site. Less commonly, symptoms like headache, flushing, nausea, or dizziness may occur. These effects are typically temporary, but it’s important to consult your healthcare provider if any reactions persist or worsen.

FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a Sermorelin injection cost?

The cost of Sermorelin injections can vary depending on the provider, dosage, and treatment plan, but typically ranges from $100 to $500 per month.

Does sermorelin need to be refrigerated?

Yes, once mixed, Sermorelin must be stored in the refrigerator to maintain its stability and effectiveness.

Is sermorelin synthetic or natural?

Sermorelin is a synthetic peptide that mimics a naturally occurring hormone called GHRH (growth hormone–releasing hormone), stimulating the body to produce more growth hormone naturally.

What precautions should be taken when using Sermorelin?

Always follow your healthcare provider’s instructions carefully. Use sterile injection techniques, store the medication properly, and report any unusual side effects or reactions. Avoid use if pregnant, breastfeeding, or if you have certain medical conditions without medical supervision.
