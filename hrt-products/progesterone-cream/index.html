About Progesterone Cream

Progesterone Cream offers a natural solution for women seeking to balance hormones gently and effectively. Specially formulated to help counteract the symptoms of hormonal imbalances, such as menopause, PMS, and other conditions related to estrogen dominance, our cream is designed for optimal absorption and efficacy. By mimicking the body's own progesterone, it supports overall hormonal health, promoting well-being and comfort during life's hormonal changes.

How does a progesterone cream work

Progesterone Cream works by delivering natural progesterone through the skin directly into the bloodstream. This method helps to mimic the body's normal hormonal rhythms, offering relief from symptoms associated with hormonal imbalances such as PMS, menopause, and conditions related to estrogen dominance. The cream helps to restore a natural balance between estrogen and progesterone levels in the body, supporting overall hormonal health.

Where do you apply progesterone cream?

Apply progesterone cream to areas where the skin is thin and can absorb it well, such as the inner arms, thighs, neck, chest, or wrists. Rotate application sites daily for best absorption.

Side effects of using progesterone cream and lotion

Progesterone Cream is generally well-tolerated, especially when used as directed. However, some individuals may experience mild side effects such as irritation at the application site, dizziness, headaches, or changes in menstrual cycles. It's rare, but if you experience any severe side effects or reactions, consult your healthcare provider immediately. Discussing your health history and any concerns with your healthcare provider can help minimize the risk of adverse effects.‍

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does it take for progesterone cream to work?

Progesterone cream may start to work within a few days to a few weeks, depending on the individual and the symptoms being treated. Full effects are often noticed after 4 to 6 weeks of consistent use.

How should I use Progesterone Cream for the best results?

For optimal results, apply the Progesterone Cream as directed by your healthcare provider, typically once or twice daily to a thin-skinned area such as the wrists, arms, chest, neck, or thighs. It's important to follow the recommended dosage and to rotate the application sites to promote even absorption. The specific regimen may vary depending on your individual health needs and the advice of your healthcare provider.

Who should use Progesterone Cream?

Progesterone Cream is suitable for women experiencing symptoms of hormonal imbalances, including those going through menopause, facing symptoms of PMS, or dealing with conditions related to estrogen dominance. It can also be used by women looking for a natural alternative to synthetic hormone replacement therapies. Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any new hormonal treatment to ensure it's appropriate for your health condition.

How do I know if I need progesterone?

You may need progesterone if you're experiencing symptoms of hormonal imbalance, such as irregular periods, mood swings, sleep issues, anxiety, low libido, or menopausal symptoms like hot flashes. A hormone test and consultation with a healthcare provider can confirm if your progesterone levels are low.
