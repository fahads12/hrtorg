FAQs about ED Treatments

What causes erectile dysfunction?

Sex and physical intimacy are an important part of overall health and wellness for most adults. Sometimes however, due to medications, a physical condition, or mental health issues, sexual health suffers. As men age, and hormone levels decline, loss of libido, erectile dysfunction may also make sex less frequent, less pleasurable, or mechanically difficult. Many times, medical treatments can restore healthy sexual function in both men and women. 31% of men report some degree of sexual dysfunction in their lifetime.

What is the fastest way to fix erectile dysfunction?

‍ Prescription medications like sildenafil (Viagra) or tadalafil (Cialis) are often the fastest-acting treatments for ED, working within 30 to 60 minutes for most men.

What's the best treatment for erectile dysfunction?

‍ The best treatment depends on the cause of ED but may include oral medications, hormone therapy, lifestyle changes, or devices like vacuum pumps. A healthcare provider can help tailor the most effective option for you.

What is the best natural supplement to treat ED?

‍ Some natural supplements that may help with ED include L-arginine, ginseng, and horny goat weed. Always consult a healthcare provider before starting supplements to ensure safety and effectiveness.

Is there something natural that works like Viagra?

‍ Certain natural remedies, such as ginseng and pomegranate juice, may improve blood flow and sexual function. While not as potent or fast-acting as Viagra, they can support erectile health when used consistently.
