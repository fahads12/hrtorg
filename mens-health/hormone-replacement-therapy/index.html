Overview

About HRT for men

As men age, sex steroid hormone levels decline, leading to a range of symptoms including mood fluctuations, decreased energy, reduced focus, and gradual weight gain. Combat the effects of aging with tailored hormone replacement therapy for men, targeting symptoms like mood swings, low energy, and decreased focus.

Testosterone decline after age 25

By age 60, roughly 30% of men meet clinical criteria for low T. Replacement restores physiological levels and often resolves the downstream symptoms.

FAQ

Common questions.

Will TRT make me infertile?

Exogenous testosterone suppresses sperm production. If fertility is a priority, we offer fertility-preserving protocols (enclomiphene, hCG, Clomid) that raise your own testosterone while maintaining sperm production. We discuss this openly before starting.

How do I know if I have low T?

Lab testing + symptoms. Total testosterone below ~300 ng/dL on two separate AM draws with symptomatic presentation (low libido, fatigue, muscle loss, mood changes) meets clinical criteria. We can order the full workup through our platform.

Is TRT safe cardiovascular-wise?

The 2023 TRAVERSE trial showed TRT in hypogonadal men did not increase major adverse cardiovascular events compared with placebo. We monitor lipids, blood pressure, hematocrit, and PSA at defined intervals.

How is this different from a steroid cycle?

TRT restores physiological testosterone levels — typically 500–900 ng/dL. Anabolic steroid use pushes levels to 1500+ ng/dL, often with multiple compounds stacked. TRT is a prescribed, monitored medical treatment with specific lab targets.
