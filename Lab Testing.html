BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Questions about choosing a kit, collecting a sample, or what comes next?

Talk to our team
How do I choose a kit?+

Compare the purpose, biomarkers, and sample type. A kit should match the question you want to discuss with your provider. The featured options measure different markers; they are not interchangeable.

What should I do before collecting a sample?+

Read the instructions supplied with your chosen kit. Collection time, menstrual-cycle timing, food, and hormone use can matter. List medicines and supplements on the requisition form, and ask your prescribing provider about any medication questions. Do not change prescribed treatment on your own.

Are all samples collected the same way?+

No. The store offers saliva, blood spot, dried urine, and combination tests. Saliva is collected in tubes; blood spots are collected on a card; dried urine uses a collection strip or card. Follow the exact procedure and drying, storage, and timing instructions supplied with your kit.

How do I return my sample?+

The store FAQs describe prepaid return packaging, and the featured thyroid and adrenal listings explicitly include a prepaid shipping label. Use your kit’s specified carrier and packaging. If a label is missing, contact support before sending your sample.

When will I receive results?+

The general store FAQs state 5–7 business days after the laboratory receives the sample. The featured thyroid and adrenal pages state 4–9 days without specifying when that period begins. Some Optimal Male/Female kit-with-consultation pages state 7–10 business days after receipt. These published estimates differ; confirm the timeline for your exact purchase with support.

Is a consultation included?+

Not with every test. The thyroid and adrenal listings describe independent physician review and personalized recommendations, which is different from a live consultation. Fundamental 5 does not specify a consultation. Confirm included services in your selected listing before ordering.

Are there shipping or location restrictions?+

The store FAQs say test kits cannot be shipped to New York or Maryland residents, California residents need prior authorization from a licensed healthcare professional, and orders cannot ship to PO boxes. Confirm current eligibility and delivery arrangements with the store before purchasing.

How do I get help with my kit or results?+

Contact HRT at info@hrt.org or (888) 574-5524. Keep your order details and kit name available so the team can help with the correct collection instructions, delivery, or results process.
