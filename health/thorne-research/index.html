Thorne Research Company Overview and Product Research

Supplemental health is becoming exceedingly important in the fast paced world we live in today. Maintaining proper nutrition is essential to ensure that your body can deal with the stress of everyday life, fight fatigue, and combat disease. Many people turn to dietary supplements for the solution. Unfortunately, many companies use potentially detrimental ingredients when manufacturing supplements and the products may contain harmful substances.

Thorne Research has made it their mission to provide dietary and sports supplements using only pure, natural, and individually tested ingredients. In addition to supplemental nutrition, they have also made it their goal to provide pertinent health information and educational materials to their consumers so you can make the best choices towards a healthier you. Numerous certifications, a vastly knowledgeable staff, and compliance with several regulatory agencies ensure that the products manufactured by Thorne are of the highest quality.

You can finally find peace of mind with your supplements because the raw ingredients that reach Thorne are put through strenuous testing from start to finish, guaranteeing that any products you consider have exceeded the toughest industry standards for quality and purity.

Dedication to Quality and Excellence

Not only has Thorne Research always maintained product standards required by the FDA Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP), they have also received recognition from the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) of Australia. TGA Australia has some of the most stringent regulatory requirements in the world for food, supplements, and other consumer products; undoubtedly, the 16 years Thorne earned an “A1” rating is a testament to their devotion towards producing pure and unpolluted dietary and nutrition supplements. Setting it even further ahead in industry standards, Thorne was proud to receive an NSF International recognition and certification for their GMPs, as well as an NSF Certified for Sport recognition for several of their nutritional supplement products.

The NSF has some of the toughest standards in testing for operating procedures and quality control documentation. If you are looking for sports supplements or nutrition, Thorne Research is the answer that millions have found, including several USA National athletes and teams.

Thorne is also one of the first nutritional supplement companies to work towards a non-GMO (Genetically Modified Organisms) Authentication from the esteemed NON-GMO Project. Thorne products are promised to be free of not only GMOs, but also gluten, artificial preservatives, stearates, wheat, rye, barley, eggs, and nuts. This Thorne Research review underlines the devotion to standards of quality by the company and staff employed there. Most Popular Products

Stress B-Complex

The Stress B-Complex by Thorne promotes adrenal support and stress management. This particular formula is superior to other products because it contains some of the B vitamins in activated form, ensuring better absorption into the body. This supplement consists of six different forms of the B vitamin and two other water soluble compounds: vitamin B1, vitamin B2, vitamin B3, vitamin B5, vitamin B6, vitamin B12, folate, and biotin. These compounds work together to promote various biochemical functions. This particular formula also contains an elevated level of the vitamin B5 to support healthy adrenal activity, a known factor in managing stress.

Everyone has something to gain from supplementing with Stress-B Complex, but it is especially useful if you suffer from compromised liver function, poorly functioning enzymes, digestive disturbances, advanced age, or other factors that prohibit your body from properly converting non-active forms of vitamins to active forms.

Vitamin D-5,000

Most people know that your body manufactures Vitamin D when your skin is exposed to the sun; however, due to seasonal changes, people residing in more Northern areas and lifestyles that no longer encourage outdoor activity, individuals are suffering from low Vitamin D levels. Vitamin D is essential for maintaining bone and muscle health and for avoiding bone-related disease. Unfortunately, a problem you may face when looking for a Vitamin D supplement is the use of ingredients with the potential to be detrimental; these include lactose, and the preservatives BHT, BHA, sodium benzoate, and sorbic acid. With Thorne products, you can always be sure that the components used are 100% pure and natural. Their Vitamin D-5,000 formula contains none of the ingredients listed above, but instead, starts with a raw, undiluted form of Vitamin D and the final mixture is made using natural plant powders and other natural components like leucine and silicon dioxide.

This supplement is NSF Certified for Sport and is intended for anyone looking to maintain optimal levels of Vitamin D. Thorne also offers Vitamin D-2,500 and Vitamin D-10,000 so you can find the supplement level best suited for your needs. This product review from Amazon.com shows how Vitamin D-5,000 can make a difference in your life.

Multi-Vitamin Elite A.M. and P.M. Formulas

This supplement isn’t your regular everyday multivitamin; it’s so powerful it had to be split into two formulas. The a.m. formula is intended to boost your energy during the day, and the p.m. formula supports restful sleep and metabolic function recovery during the night. A solid multivitamin is the ground stone for your supplemental needs. It should ensure that, even with the poor food choices promoted by today’s food industry, you are getting all of your necessary daily nutrients. The Journal of the American Medical Association agrees that most adults don’t get enough nutrition from their everyday diets alone and should take at least a multivitamin supplement.¹

The Multi-vitamin elite system from Thorne is an NSF Certified for Sport supplement and contains everything you need and more to support daily biochemical functions and overall health.
