Mood & Hormonal SupportMaintaining the right balance of hormones is a vital key to your health and wellness. Explore the leading products today!Maintenance:

EPA

EPA is a concentrated formula that contains the omega-3 essential fatty acid EPA, which helps promote positive mood and well-being.* In fact, Nordic Naturals EPA, which provides 850 mg EPA per serving, is clinically shown to support a healthy mood. EPA also helps maintain a healthy heart and circulatory system, supports joint mobility, and encourages proper immune system function.*Being proactive about your health can help prevent disease and other health related issues we experience later in life.Proactive:

Complete Omega-D3™

Complete Omega-D3 blends EPA and DHA from fish oil with omega-6 GLA from borage oil, and omega-9. An added 1000 I.U. of vitamin D3 per serving is in the natural form produced by the body (cholecalciferol), which is better absorbed and utilized than other forms.* This omega-rich blend offers balanced support for a healthy heart, brain function, a positive mood, plus healthy joints and skin.*High Intensity:

Nordic GLA™

Nordic GLA is a vegetarian omega oil made exclusively from non-GMO New Zealand-grown borage seed. Formulated from the unique omega-6 gamma-linolenic acid, it offers natural, effective support for healthy skin, joints, and a positive mood.* It is an ideal companion to any Nordic Naturals omega-3 product.

Omega Woman®

Omega Woman is an evening primrose supplement with a touch of omega-3 from fish oil to support a woman’s unique hormonal needs and radiant skin health.* It is an ideal companion to any Nordic Naturals omega-3 product.
