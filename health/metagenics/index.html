Metagenics Company Review

Health and fitness has become an increasingly popular topic of conversation over the past few years. People are finding worth in investing in their overall health. They realize that eating properly, exercising on a regular basis, and taking supplements that will provide the proper vitamins and nutrients is actually worth it. With this healthy trend comes a blast of products offering different health benefits. At times, it can be overwhelming to sort through which ones will actually be worth the investment.

We’re here today to provide an information guide to Metagenics and their innovative product line. Metagenics products have become the leading physician prescribed vitamins and healthy supplement brand on the market. This is due largely because of their natural ingredients that are perfectly formulated to give the body what it needs. Many vitamins today are filled with synthetic or filler ingredients. They don’t do any good for the body and can at times create additional problems. Metagenics product line seeks to remove these unnecessary filler ingredients and focus on making products that do nothing but good for your body.

Metagenics Review

Metagenics has been around as a supplement manufacturer for roughly thirty years. They are well established in the medical field and trusted by many physicians as a natural way to implement nutrigenomics. We bring this Metagenics review to you so you can be informed about who this company is and why it receives such applause.

The biggest noteworthy thing to mention about Metagenics is its dedication to providing outstanding, high-quality ingredients to their customers. They lauded in the field of nutrigenomics, which is the study of using supplements to maintain health.

When you’re looking to decide on a product line, it’s important to find one that is well established and reputable in the medical field. You want to make sure you’re putting whole ingredients in your body that will nourish and support your immune and digestive health. Metagenics products not only meet a high industry rating, but are pharmaceutical quality,  formulated with ingredients that have been clinically studied to improve an individual's’ overall health over time with regular use.

What Is Metagenics?

Metagenics is a company that is seeking to offer people nutritional support and relief from chronic health conditions. These conditions such as obesity, diabetes, heart disease and high blood pressure have become extremely common, especially in the United States. Metagenics believes that these chronic health conditions are preventable. Often a result of bad eating habits, a sedentary lifestyle, or a life that is too full of stress and extra demands.

Metagenics’ primary goal is to help people find a healthier lifestyle. They have developed a Therapeutic Lifestyle Change (TLC) program that is designed to help people take control of their health. Often, people do not know what they’re doing wrong or how to reverse the problem. The TLC program seeks to educate people with long-term solutions to problems. They don’t guarantee overnight results, but they do guarantee results with sustained hard work.

In addition to this program designed and implemented by highly educated healthcare professionals, Metagenics has developed and released an entire line of products that are designed to help people regain and maintain their health.

Top Metagenics Products

If you’re unfamiliar with Metagenics’ impressive product line, we’ve put together this top 5 list to help familiarize you with some of their most popular products. Metagenics customer reviews are ample about these products, and very positive. All five of these products have studies proven to show their provide great results and have made a noticeable, positive impact on customers’ health. All of these receive a very high Metagenics rating as well as high Metagenics customer reviews.

UltraFlora Balance

Digestive issues are often linked back to an imbalance of beneficial intestinal flora. When this flora becomes unbalanced, the immune system cannot properly function and your body struggles with digestion. UltraFlora Balance seeks to offer immune and digestive support with probiotics. There are two viable strains of probiotics in this product: L/ acidophilus NCFM and B. lactis Bi-07. The combination of the two of these is what offers immune support and overall digestive wellness. You can also use this product if you feel a cold coming on, or as a preventative measure during flu season. This product is gluten and dairy free.

Mag Glycinate

Everybody needs a healthy balance of magnesium in their bodies. Magnesium supports a healthy heart, muscle and nervous system, and strong bones. This supplement provides your body with proper levels of magnesium amino acid chelate. It’s specially formulated so that your intestines can properly absorb the magnesium and use it most beneficially. Mag Glycinate is sometimes prescribed by medical professionals as a way of reducing the occurrence of muscle and joint cramps.

OmegaGenics EPA-DHA 720

This product sounds like a character from Star Wars but it’s actually designed to keep your heart, brain, joints and circulatory system as healthy as possible! These fish oils are jam-packed with omega-3 fatty acids, pulled from cold water fish, naturally sourced in Norway. You’ll notice these softgels have a slight lemon flavor which is made of natural ingredients. If your cholesterol or triglyceride levels are high, consult with your doctor as this supplement can be especially helpful in maintaining proper healthy levels.

PhytoMulti Without Iron 120 Tablets

A healthy balance of vitamins and minerals as we age is paramount, a leading product by Metagenics is PhytoMulti Without Iron. They also offer this product with iron that can be found here.

D35000

Not all of us get enough Vitamin D and that is not a good thing! Vitamin D is necessary to help you think clearly, and have energy to get through your day. Also, the more Vitamin D your skin has, the healthier you’ll appear. D3 5000 is formulated to provide high levels of vitamin D3 that is easy for your body to absorb and use immediately.

Vessel Care

Cardiovascular health is a huge problem in the United States. People are consuming unhealthy foods on a regular basis, causing damage to arterial walls and eventually clogging the arteries. This plaque that builds up is caused by rising homocysteine levels. Vessel Care is designed to combat this build-up by maintaining healthy levels of homocysteine in your body. This contributes to your overall cardiovascular health, keep you and your heart healthy and happy.

As mentioned earlier, Metagenics is committed to providing supplements that not only nourish our bodies, but seek to heal. If you’re interested in these products, it’s recommended to consult with a physician before starting on a supplement regimen. You always want to make sure that the supplements you’re taking will interact properly with each other and your body’s natural chemistry. If budget is a concern, consider asking your doctor if they have any sort of arrangement with the company where they could provide some Metagenics coupons or some way of saving some money. However, it's important to remember that our health is worth investing in when possible, with or without Metagenics coupons.

The path to health is possible for everyone, with a little time, research and motivation. Enjoy your journey of health and discovery as you pursue how Metagenics might be a healthy and positive choice for you and your family.

Other Popular Metagenics Health Supplements

The Metagenics health supplements product line is broad and well loved by millions of people. Their products are gluten free and non-GMO, seeking to offer maximum health benefits to everyone.

Endefen: Multi-Dimensional GI Support & Protection

Endefen is one of the most popular Metagenics health supplements. It comes in powder form and is designed to make sure the gastrointestinal (GI) tract operates and functions properly. Made of a unique blend of ingredients, this formula promotes healthy growth of gastric mucosal cells. These gastric mucosal cells contribute to keeping your gastric lining intact and functioning properly.

Studies have shown that people who regularly consume a proper balance of lecithin and astaxanthin have a healthier gastric environment, as it is more protected from negative antibodies. Other ingredients in Endefen include green tea which promotes the growth of healthy intestinal microflora. This ensures your digestive system runs regularly and smoothly.

UltraCare for Kids

Keeping our children healthy is one of the most important things we can strive to do. Side from ensuring they’re being fed well-rounded meals full of health, nutrient-dense ingredients, it’s also helpful to give them an additional boost of support. UltraCare for Kids is designed specifically for children. Made of a blend of micro and macronutrients, UltraCare offers nutritional support to keep growing kids as healthy as possible.

If your child has a restricted diet due to an allergy or sensitivity, UltraCare can be a great way to get them some extra nutrition. This supplement is dairy free but chock full of calcium to help your child form healthy bones. It also contains fructooligosaccharides which support the growth of beneficial bacteria in your child’s gut.

Adreset

We all lead very busy lives and with that often comes an immense amount of stress. When stress builds up for too long over an extended period of time, it can cause both mental and physical weariness. Adreset is designed to combat this problem in a natural way.

The unique combination of cordyceps mycelium extra, asian ginseng, and rhodiola root extract works to invigorate the body. It’s prescribed to take two capsules, twice daily for best results. The product is 100% gluten free, non-GMO and vegetarian. Adreset will help you over time to build up a resilience against stress. It’s recommended to take these regularly in order to combat stress as it occurs.
