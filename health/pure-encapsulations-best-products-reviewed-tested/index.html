Top Products

Here are some of their top products. These supplements can only be purchased from certain online merchants and certified medical practitioners. One can use promo codes or coupons for discounts on certain brands when purchasing from these sellers.

Magnesium Glycinate

Although Magnesium (Mg) is one of the insufficient serum electrolyte, it is quite vital for the metabolism of a number of minerals. Note that a deficiency of magnesium can cause diseases that lead to massive suffering. Some of the diseases associated with it are glaucoma, hypertension, Alzheimer’s disease, multiple sclerosis, and many more.

Magnesium Glycinate is a reliable form of Magnesium that depends little on the normal means of digestion to be absorbed well. The glycine salt it contains can be absorbed by the body through channels such as those used by amino acids. Magnesium activates the enzymes needed for several physiological functions like the cardiac function. It’s necessary for energy production and the metabolism of fats, carbohydrates, and amino acids.

B Complex Plus

This product is considered a plus. It contains synergists such as Thiamin (b1), Riboflavin (b2), Pantothenic Acid (b5), and Methylcoblamin (b12), among other essential nutrients. This B Complex Plus contains Pyridoxal 5 Phosphate (P5P), an activated form of B6, which is easier to absorb compared to other B6 supplements.

In their complex form, B Vitamins are necessary for the proper functioning of the nervous system. Moreover, B vitamins are essential in the conversion of carbohydrates into energy, the maintenance of muscle tone in the GI tract, and the metabolism of protein and fats. These vitamins also support the coherence of the liver, the hair, and the skin.

Nutrient 950

This supplement contains numerous vitamins and minerals that are beneficial. From its excellent customer reviews on Amazon, it’s clearly one of the top supplements. It comprises of Vitamin K as a blend of K1, K2, and K4 to allow absolute nutrient absorption. Vitamin K is essential in de-calcifying the vascular system.

Vitamin K2 encourages osteocalcin to bind the bones and contribute to healthy body mineralization. As MK-7, it is necessary for maintaining healthy arterial calcium metabolism, which supports a healthy vascular elasticity. Taking supplements that ensure the body gets enough Vitamin K goes a long way in improving one’s vascular health.
