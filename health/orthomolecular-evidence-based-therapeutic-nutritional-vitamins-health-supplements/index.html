Orthomolecular Company Overview and Popular Product Review

There are different kinds of health supplements in the market today, each promising a different result. Dietary supplements that aid in either weight loss or the increase of specific nutritional content in your body and medical supplements that are used to improve a particular set of symptoms also exist.

Orthomolecular is one of the leading manufacturers of health supplements of a wide variety. They produce hypoallergenic, blood pressure, vision and a number of other supplements. If you are wondering where to buy these supplements, visit their website or a drug store and do so with a prescription from a doctor.

The Company Overview

Orthomolecular is a pharmaceutical company that has been in existence for a long time. The market target for the company is mainly certified doctors, hospitals, and pharmacies.

Also, orthomolecular product formulations involve evidence-based research combined with quality raw materials, which produce effective supplements that strive to improve the patient’s health. The evidence comes from well-controlled and carefully researched human trials that have been published in scientific journals. Beyond the human trials, the company also gets its evidence from historical data and by observing health care practitioners in their jobs.

The company also boasts high-quality products that are FDA approved and compliant with set standards and methods of procedure. This quality is assured by the use of superior raw materials and therapeutic potency. The raw materials are chosen carefully by evaluating whether they match the specifications stated in clinical trials. Therapeutic potency is the process of ensuring that the raw materials are not only of a high quality but are also mixed in the correct dosages. If too little raw material is used, the supplement cannot be effective.

Unlike most dietary supplement companies that outsource their products, OrthoMolecular manufactures its own. The company runs a manufacturing facility in Wisconsin and operates the whole production process right from formulation to bottling and labeling. Besides, the manufacturing facility adheres to the set cGMPs standards and has passed a number of third-party audits. There is extensive testing carried out on every product that is manufactured to assure purity, strength, and therapeutic potency.

The company attempts to ensure that their service is top-notch. Its various departments coordinate to ensure that they accurately respond to your inquiries and deliver your products in time.

Let’s Review Some Popular Products

Below is a product review of the popular supplements produced by the company:

1) Diaxinol

There is a wide variety of Alpha-Lipoic Acid Supplements in the market today and Diaxinol is one of them. These vitamins all attempt to do one task; aid in the conversion of glucose to energy. Some studies have shown that it has the capacity to improve insulin sensitivity and control blood sugar for people with type 2 diabetes.

Diaxinol is also used to treat memory loss, chronic fatigue syndrome, and diseases of the heart and blood vessels, among others. It works by preventing cell damage in the body and restoring the levels of vitamin E and C. Moreover, it breaks down carbohydrates into energy for other organs of the body to use.

The supplement is safe for adults when consumed orally, taken intravenously, or applied to the skin. However, people with diabetes should check their consumption of the supplement as it might lower their blood sugar. Also, pregnant or breastfeeding women should not use it as not enough is known about its side effects. Finally, ensure that you don’t use the supplement while taking a lot of alcohol. The reason is that alcohol reduces the level of thiamine in the blood and taking Diaxinal when these concentrations are low may lead to health complications.

2) Nattokinase by Ortho

Nattokinase supplements are produced to reduce blood pressure, the risk of blood clots, stroke, chest pain, and other diseases of the heart and circulatory system. They can also be used to alleviate pain, chronic fatigue syndrome, and beriberi, among others. Ortho produces Nattokinase supplements known as Nattokinase that come in soft gel capsules. The containers they are packed in vary in size, and the company has 45 and 90 capsule variants.

The enzyme works by thinning blood, and that reduces its ability to clot. That ensures that there is a reduced chance of getting diseases caused by blood clots like stroke and heart attacks. It is safe to take the medicine in amounts similar to the ones found in foods with natto, like the soy food natto. When taking the supplement orally, ensure that you don’t take more than two doses to be on the safe side.

However, there are situations where you should avoid nattokinase supplements. First, women who are pregnant or breastfeeding should avoid the supplement as there is not enough information about the possible side effects to them or the unborn fetuses. Also, anyone suffering from a bleeding disorder or an individual who has just undergone surgery should not take these vitamins as it may cause increased bleeding.

3) CLA

The second supplement on this product review is the Conjugated Linoleic Acid supplement. it is a fatty acid produced by Ortho and the aim of this product is to aid in weight loss by increasing the ratio of muscle to fat. Since it is quite costly a product, make certain that you get value for your money.

CLA is a fatty acid that can be naturally found in meat and dairy products but is sold as a supplement by this company. It promises to help you lose fat, maintain a constant weight loss pattern, retain lean muscle mass, and control type 2 diabetes by increasing the body’s metabolism. The supplement is sold as a soft gel capsule and is most effective when combined with moderate exercise.

If you are a doctor and are wondering where to buy FDA-approved health supplements, you can get them from Orthomolecular Products. With coupons, you can qualify for discounts off the prices of the supplements from the company. The review above covers some of the most popular supplements manufactured by the pharmaceutical firm.
