The care journey

Understand what comes next.
One conversation at a time.

Five parts of an informed care journey. The order and testing needs can vary with your service and evaluation.

01

Explore your concerns

Notice what has changed.

Think about your symptoms, health goals, and the questions you want to ask. Explore hormone health, weight management, sexual health, hair loss, and other treatment information.

Symptom assessments can help you organize your concerns; they do not provide a diagnosis.

02

Provide your health history

Bring the details that matter.

Have your medical history, current medications and supplements, allergies, and any relevant lab reports ready. The patient portal is the place to continue with the care flow.

Our local consultation-preparation page helps you get organized. It does not submit a medical intake or book an appointment.

03

Complete testing when appropriate

The right test depends on the question.

Testing may inform an evaluation. Explore the available profiles and collection methods, then review the exact kit listing before purchasing through the HRT store.

If you use a kit, follow its supplied collection, timing, packaging, and return instructions. Testing is not a required purchase for every care pathway.

04

Discuss results and options

Make room for a conversation.

Review your symptoms, history, and any available results with your provider. Discuss whether a proposed treatment is suitable, its possible risks and side effects, and alternatives.

An evaluation does not guarantee a prescription. A test purchase and a live consultation are not automatically the same service.

05

Follow your care plan

Keep the conversation going.

If treatment is prescribed, follow the instructions given by your provider. Some services describe prescription delivery and ongoing monitoring; arrangements depend on your specific treatment and plan.

Confirm delivery, follow-up, repeat testing, and any associated fees with your care team. Bring new questions or changes in symptoms to their attention.

Questions about the process

Know what
to expect.

Need help with a specific order or service?

Contact the HRT team →
Where should I start: testing or a consultation?+

Start with the questions you want answered. You can explore test kits independently, subject to product and location requirements, or enter the provider-care flow to discuss your concerns. These are different starting points, not one mandatory purchase sequence.

Do I need lab testing before treatment?+

It depends on the service and individual evaluation. Hormone-related care may involve testing, while other services describe health-history review and a virtual consultation first. Discuss which tests, if any, are appropriate before ordering.

What should I prepare before a consultation?+

Bring a description of your symptoms and when they began, your goals, relevant medical history, allergies, current medications and supplements, and any available lab reports. You can use our consultation-preparation page before continuing to the patient portal.

Does every test kit include a consultation?+

No universal consultation inclusion is established. Some products are specifically sold with consultation, while other listings describe analysis or physician review without specifying a live visit. Check the exact product listing and confirm what is included before purchasing.

How will I receive my test results?+

The store describes electronic results through secure access. When a provider orders testing, results may be sent to that provider. Follow the access instructions for your purchase; the laboratory results account may differ from the HRT patient portal.

Does a consultation guarantee treatment or a prescription?+

No. A provider evaluates your circumstances before recommending care. Use the consultation to discuss suitability, risks, alternatives, and any further evaluation that may be needed.

Where do I purchase a test kit?+

Purchases take place in the separate HRT store. Review the chosen profile, collection instructions, shipping eligibility, current price, and purchase inclusions there before checkout.

How do I access the patient portal?+

Use the patient-portal links on this page. Get started opens the HRT symptoms-tracker route; Open the patient portal takes you to the portal entry page. The local preparation guide does not create an account or submit an intake.

What happens after a treatment plan is agreed upon?+

Your next steps depend on the treatment prescribed and the plan you select. Confirm how to use the treatment, delivery arrangements, monitoring, follow-up, and any additional costs with your care team.

How long does testing take, and what will care cost?+

Timing and fees vary by purchase. The store general FAQs give a 5–7 business-day estimate after sample receipt, while some individual listings give different estimates. Review the kit-specific information and confirm current fees and inclusions before paying.

Where is telehealth care available?+

HRT.org currently lists telehealth services in Florida and California. Test-kit shipping has separate eligibility requirements. Confirm availability for your location and chosen service before starting.
