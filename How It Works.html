The care journey

Understand what comes next.
One conversation at a time.

These five steps explain how care can work. The order and any testing you need depend on your service and evaluation.

YOUR CARE JOURNEY 01 / 05

Explore your concerns

01

Explore your concerns

Notice what has changed.

Think about your symptoms, health goals, and the questions you want to ask. Explore hormone health, weight management, sexual health, hair loss, and other treatment information.

Symptom assessments can help you organize your concerns; they do not provide a diagnosis.

Explore symptom assessments →
02

Provide your health history

Bring the details that matter.

Have your medical history, current medications and supplements, allergies, and relevant lab reports ready. Continue your care through the patient portal.

Use the consultation-preparation page to organize your notes. It does not submit a medical intake or book an appointment.

Prepare for a consultation →
03

Complete testing when appropriate

The right test depends on the question.

Testing may help with an evaluation. Compare the available test profiles and collection methods, then read the listing for your chosen kit before buying it from the HRT store.

If you use a kit, follow its instructions for collection, timing, packaging, and returns. You do not need to buy a test for every type of care.

Explore test kits & instructions →
04

Discuss results and options

Make room for a conversation.

Go over your symptoms, health history, and any results with your provider. Ask whether the proposed treatment is suitable for you, what risks and side effects it may have, and what alternatives are available.

An evaluation does not guarantee a prescription. Buying a test does not automatically include a live consultation.

Continue in the patient portal →
05

Follow your care plan

Keep the conversation going.

If your provider prescribes treatment, follow their instructions. Some services describe prescription delivery and ongoing monitoring. The arrangements depend on your treatment and plan.

Ask your care team about delivery, follow-up, repeat tests, and any fees. Let them know about new questions or changes in your symptoms.

Questions about the process

Know what
to expect.

Need help with a specific order or service?

Contact the HRT team →
Where should I start: testing or a consultation?+

Start with the questions you want answered. You can browse test kits on your own, subject to product and location requirements, or speak with a provider about your concerns. You do not have to purchase them in a fixed order.

Do I need lab testing before treatment?+

It depends on the service and your evaluation. Hormone-related care may involve testing. Other services describe a health-history review and virtual consultation first. Ask which tests, if any, are appropriate before ordering.

What should I prepare before a consultation?+

Bring a description of your symptoms and when they began, your goals, relevant medical history, allergies, current medications and supplements, and any available lab reports. You can use our consultation-preparation page before continuing to the patient portal.

Does every test kit include a consultation?+

A consultation is not included with every kit. Some products are sold with a consultation. Others list analysis or physician review without specifying a live visit. Read your chosen product’s listing and confirm what is included before purchasing.

How will I receive my test results?+

The store describes secure online access to results. If a provider orders your test, the results may be sent to them. Follow the access instructions that come with your purchase. Your laboratory results account may be separate from the HRT patient portal.

Does a consultation guarantee treatment or a prescription?+

No. A provider evaluates your circumstances before recommending care. Use the consultation to discuss suitability, risks, alternatives, and any further evaluation that may be needed.

Where do I purchase a test kit?+

Buy your kit in the separate HRT store. Before checkout, check the test profile, collection instructions, shipping eligibility, current price, and what is included.

How do I access the patient portal?+

Use the patient-portal links on this page. Get started takes you to the HRT symptom tracker; Open the patient portal takes you to the portal’s entry page. The preparation guide does not create an account or submit an intake.

What happens after a treatment plan is agreed upon?+

What happens next depends on your prescribed treatment and chosen plan. Ask your care team how to use the treatment, how delivery works, what monitoring and follow-up you’ll need, and whether there are additional costs.

How long does testing take, and what will care cost?+

Timing and fees depend on what you buy. The store’s general FAQs estimate 5–7 business days after the sample is received, but some product listings give different estimates. Check your kit’s information and confirm current fees and what is included before paying.

Where is telehealth care available?+

HRT.org currently lists telehealth services in Florida and California. Test kits have separate shipping requirements. Before starting, check whether your chosen service is available where you live.
