How do I know which treatment to explore?+

Start with the symptoms or concerns you want to discuss. The treatment categories above offer background information; a provider reviews your history and helps determine which options are appropriate. A symptom assessment is not a diagnosis.

Will I need lab testing before treatment?+

Hormone-related care may include laboratory testing. Other services begin with a health-history review or consultation. The appropriate evaluation depends on the service and your individual circumstances.

Are treatments available for both women and men?+

HRT.org provides separate information for women and men across these five treatment areas. Choose the relevant information above, then discuss your particular needs and eligibility with a provider.

Does a consultation guarantee a prescription?+

No. Treatment recommendations and any prescription depend on a provider's assessment. Discuss your medical history, current medications, possible side effects, and alternatives during your consultation.

What should I ask about before starting?+

Ask about the purpose of the proposed treatment, potential risks and side effects, alternatives, how it is used, and what monitoring or follow-up is needed. Confirm costs and what is included before purchasing.

Where can I purchase an at-home test?+

At-home test kits are sold through the separate HRT store. Visit Lab Testing to explore collection methods and featured kits, then use the store to review product-specific details, shipping eligibility, and checkout.
