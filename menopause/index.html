What is menopause and how HRT (Hormone Replacement Therapy) can help?

Menopause is a natural transition that every woman experiences, yet it can bring a host of challenging symptoms. Did you know that approximately 1.3 million women in the U.S. enter menopause each year? Despite its prevalence, many women struggle to find the right support and treatment. Our telehealth services bridge this gap by providing convenient, expert care tailored to your unique needs.

85%

of postmenopausal women have experienced menopause-related symptoms in their lifetime.

Symptoms including mood swings, hot flashes, low energy, reduced concentration, and weight gain are common to both sexes.

 HRT can help dealing with the following symptoms during menopause

  • Hot Flashes
  • Night Sweats
  • Mood Swings
  • Vaginal Dryness
  • Sleep Disturbances
  • Fatigue
  • Low Libido
  • Weight Gain
  • Brain Fog
  • Irritability
  • Hair Loss
  • Loss of Bone Density
  • Changes in Skin
  • Reduced Muscle Mass
  • Joint Discomfort
