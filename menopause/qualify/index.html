About You

What's your age?

HRT is most beneficial when started within 10 years of menopause or under age 60. We'll ask a short series of clinical questions to see if HRT is right for you.

Cycle Status

Where are you in the menopausal transition?

This affects what we prescribe (progesterone-only, combined, continuous vs. cyclic).

Symptoms

Which symptoms are you experiencing?

Select all that apply. Vasomotor symptoms (hot flashes, night sweats) respond most reliably to HRT.

Surgical History

Have you had a hysterectomy?

If your uterus is intact, systemic estrogen must be paired with progesterone to protect the endometrium.
