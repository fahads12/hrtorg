THYROID

Thyroid shapes everything. And TSH alone misses a lot.

Energy, metabolism, mood, hair, body temperature — all downstream of thyroid state. We test the full panel (TSH, free T3, free T4, TPOab) because 'normal' TSH doesn't rule out treatable thyroid dysfunction.
