For Women

Symptoms most commonly tied to the female endocrine shift.

Symptom

Hot flashes

Vasomotor shifts in perimenopause and beyond. HRT resolves them for 90% of women within 2–8 weeks.

See the full guide
Symptom

Night sweats

Hot flashes at night can disrupt sleep. Treatment addresses the same cause as daytime hot flashes, with added attention to sleep.

See the full guide
Symptom

Vaginal dryness

Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM) — dryness, painful intercourse, recurrent UTIs. Vaginal estrogen is highly effective.

See the full guide
Symptom

Mood changes & anxiety

Perimenopausal estrogen and progesterone shifts move serotonin and GABA. Often responds better to HRT than SSRIs.

See the full guide
Symptom

Irregular periods

Shorter cycles, skipped periods, heavier flow, or new spotting can be early signs of perimenopause, PCOS, or thyroid changes.

See the full guide
For Men

Symptoms most commonly tied to the male endocrine shift.

Symptom

Erectile dysfunction

Treatment depends on vascular, hormonal, and neurological causes. Options include PDE5 inhibitors, TRT, Trimix, and shockwave therapy.

See the full guide
Symptom

Low energy & fatigue

In men, often tied to low testosterone, thyroid dysfunction, or cortisol dysregulation. We test the stack.

See the full guide
Symptom

Poor recovery

Slower rebound from training is an endocrine signal. Testosterone, GH axis, cortisol, thyroid all shape it.

See the full guide
Symptom

Muscle loss (sarcopenia)

Age-related loss of lean mass accelerates with hormonal decline. TRT + strength training protects it.

See the full guide
Everyone

Symptoms that affect both audiences — the drivers vary, the workup overlaps.

Symptom

Brain fog

Word-finding, memory, slowed thinking. Estrogen, thyroid, cortisol, and insulin resistance all drive it.

See the full guide
Symptom

Poor sleep

Trouble falling or staying asleep. Progesterone, cortisol rhythm, thyroid, and melatonin all show up here.

See the full guide
Symptom

Weight changes

Metabolic shifts resistant to diet. Thyroid, insulin, cortisol, and sex hormones all shape body composition.

See the full guide
Symptom

Low libido

Reduced desire in women and men. Testosterone, estrogen, DHEA, and thyroid state all contribute.

See the full guide
Symptom

Hair loss

Thinning or shedding can involve androgens, thyroid, iron, protein, or hormone changes. Treatment depends on the pattern and sex.

See the full guide
Symptom

Anxiety

New or worsening anxious symptoms. Progesterone drops, thyroid overactivity, cortisol spikes all present this way.

See the full guide
Symptom

Depression

Mood changes related to hypothyroidism, declining estrogen during perimenopause, or clinically low testosterone.

See the full guide
Symptom

Bone, muscle & joint pain

Aches, stiffness, and reduced bone density track with hormone decline. Estrogen and testosterone protect bone, muscle, and joint integrity.

See the full guide
Symptom

Skin changes

Dryness, thinning, loss of elasticity, and new sensitivity are common hormonal signals in perimenopause and andropause.

See the full guide
