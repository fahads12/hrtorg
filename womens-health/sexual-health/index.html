Sex and physical intimacy are an important part of overall health and wellness for most adults.

  • Sometimes however, due to medications, a physical condition, or mental health issues, sexual health suffers.
  • As women age, and hormone levels decline, loss of libido, or painful intercourse may also make sex less frequent, less pleasurable, or mechanically difficult.
  • Many times, medical treatments can restore healthy sexual function in women.

43% of women report some degree of sexual dysfunction in their lifetime.

How do I know if I am sexually healthy? Are you dealing with these symptoms?

  • Lack of Interest in Sex
  • Difficulty Becoming Aroused
  • Painful Intercourse

Proven results to maintain a sexual health

  • Lower Blood Pressure: Enhanced sexual health can help with relaxation and stress relief, which may in turn help lower blood pressure.
  • Decrease in Triglyceride Levels: Regular sexual activity can contribute to overall cardiovascular health, potentially leading to lower levels of triglycerides in the blood.
  • Lower Risk of Heart Disease: Improved sexual health can lead to better blood flow and reduced stress, both of which are beneficial for heart health.
  • Increased Mobility: Improving sexual health can encourage more physical engagement and activity, potentially leading to better overall mobility.
  • Reduction in Pain: Addressing sexual health issues can sometimes alleviate discomfort associated with sexual activity, making it more enjoyable.
  • Increased Self-esteem: Overcoming sexual health challenges can boost confidence and overall self-esteem.
  • Improved Insulin Resistance: Enhanced sexual health and activity may offer overall health benefits, including potentially better insulin sensitivity, although the effects are likely indirect.
  • Improved Mood: A fulfilling sexual life often leads to an emotional uplift, partly due to the endorphins released during sexual activity.

How to maintain your sexual health?

Maintain your sexual health by practicing safe sex, getting regular STI screenings, staying informed, communicating openly with partners, and caring for your physical and mental well-being.

FAQs about women and sexual health

How do I know if I am sexually healthy? Are you dealing with these symptoms?

Lack of Interest in Sex Difficulty Becoming Aroused Painful Intercourse

How to maintain your sexual health?

Maintain your sexual health by practicing safe sex, getting regular STI screenings, staying informed, communicating openly with partners, and caring for your physical and mental well-being.

What is the most common sexual problem for females?

Low sexual desire, also known as low libido, is the most common sexual issue among females. It can be influenced by stress, hormonal changes, medical conditions, or relationship factors.

How can I improve my sexual health naturally?

Eat a balanced diet, exercise regularly, reduce stress, get enough sleep, avoid smoking or excessive alcohol, and maintain open communication with your partner. These habits support both physical and emotional aspects of sexual health.

How can I get my sexual health back?

Focus on healthy lifestyle changes, address any underlying health issues, manage stress, and seek support from a healthcare provider or therapist if needed. Rebuilding sexual health takes time, self-care, and sometimes professional help.

What is considered sexual wellness?

Sexual wellness is a state of physical, emotional, mental, and social well-being related to sexuality. It includes safe, respectful, and pleasurable sexual experiences, as well as knowledge and autonomy over sexual health.
