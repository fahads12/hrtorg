# Redesign assets

All molecular and testing-process illustrations are original inline SVG/CSS, with no third-party licenses or paid services required. They are conceptual diagrams, not product photographs or representations of kit contents.

Logo: user-supplied `Crisp HRT Logo on Transparent Background.png`, found in Downloads and copied unchanged to `uploads/hrt-logo.png`. Original proportions and transparency are preserved.

The two PNG files in uploads are full-page design mockups. They are not used as verified product photography. Original kit photos now come from HRT.org. Purchasing links use the separate Shopify store at https://store.hrt.org/; no local cart or checkout is built.

The care sequence covers intake, provider review, testing, delivery, and ongoing care. The testing presentation now uses the original Lab Testing page's ordering, collection and prepaid-return information. Turnaround differs between original pages, so the overview avoids a timing promise.

Original kit photography, provider portraits and article images were collected from public HRT.org pages for this authorized redesign. Images are optimized to WebP, at most 1200 × 1000, quality 82. Exact source URLs, local files and rights context appear in `source-content/asset-manifest.json`. Original website ownership is relied upon; no independent third-party commercial license is asserted.

Component research included [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/) and the [shadcn/ui MIT license](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Native details/summary accordions and original CSS were implemented; no third-party component code was copied.

Geist and Newsreader remain served through Google Fonts. Local production React/ReactDOM distributions retain their license notices. No paid assets, external generation credits or purchases were used.
