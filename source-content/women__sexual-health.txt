FAQs about women and sexual health
What is the most common sexual problem for females?
Low sexual desire, also known as low libido, is the most common sexual issue among females. It can be influenced by stress, hormonal changes, medical conditions, or relationship factors.
How can I improve my sexual health naturally?
Eat a balanced diet, exercise regularly, reduce stress, get enough sleep, avoid smoking or excessive alcohol, and maintain open communication with your partner. These habits support both physical and emotional aspects of sexual health.
How can I get my sexual health back?
Focus on healthy lifestyle changes, address any underlying health issues, manage stress, and seek support from a healthcare provider or therapist if needed. Rebuilding sexual health takes time, self-care, and sometimes professional help.
What is considered sexual wellness?
Sexual wellness is a state of physical, emotional, mental, and social well-being related to sexuality. It includes safe, respectful, and pleasurable sexual experiences, as well as knowledge and autonomy over sexual health.
