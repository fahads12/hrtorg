Who should not take Viagra?
Viagra is not suitable for everyone. People taking nitrates for chest pain, those with severe heart or liver problems, or those who have had recent strokes or heart attacks should not take Viagra. It's also not recommended for those allergic to sildenafil or any of the tablet's other ingredients. Always consult a healthcare provider to ensure Viagra is safe for you.
Are there any side effects associated with taking Viagra?
Common side effects of Viagra include headaches, facial flushing, indigestion, nasal congestion, dizziness, and visual changes such as increased sensitivity to light or blurred vision. These side effects are usually mild and temporary. However, if you experience severe side effects like an erection lasting more than 4 hours (priapism), sudden vision loss, or chest pain, seek immediate medical attention.
What is the generic Viagra for men?
The generic Viagra for men is called sildenafil. It works by increasing blood flow to help achieve and maintain an erection and is typically taken 30–60 minutes before sexual activity.
