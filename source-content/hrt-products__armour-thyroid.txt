What is Armour Thyroid and how is it different from synthetic thyroid medications?
Armour Thyroid is a natural thyroid hormone replacement medication made from desiccated porcine (pig) thyroid glands. It contains a combination of both T4 (thyroxine) and T3 (triiodothyronine) hormones, which are identical to the hormones produced by the human thyroid gland. This contrasts with many synthetic thyroid medications, which often only contain T4. Armour Thyroid is designed to provide a more natural approach to thyroid hormone replacement, offering both T4 and T3 to support thyroid health.
‍
How to take Armour Thyroid?
Armour Thyroid is taken orally, usually once daily in the morning on an empty stomach, 30 to 60 minutes before breakfast. Follow your healthcare provider’s dosage instructions closely and avoid taking calcium or iron supplements within four hours of the dose, as they may affect absorption.
‍
Are there any side effects associated with Armour Thyroid?
While Armour Thyroid is generally well-tolerated, some individuals may experience side effects, especially if the dose is not correctly adjusted. Potential side effects can include heart palpitations, increased appetite, weight loss, sweating, and nervousness. It's important to start with a low dose and gradually adjust based on your body's response and thyroid hormone levels, under the guidance of your healthcare provider.

Who should consider using Armour Thyroid?
Armour Thyroid is suitable for individuals diagnosed with hypothyroidism (an underactive thyroid) who are looking for a natural alternative to synthetic thyroid hormone replacement. It may be particularly beneficial for patients who have not found relief from symptoms of hypothyroidism with synthetic T4-only medications, as Armour Thyroid includes both T4 and T3 hormones.
