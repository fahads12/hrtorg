What are Progesterone Capsules used for?
Progesterone Capsules are used to supplement the body’s natural progesterone levels, addressing deficiencies that can lead to menstrual irregularities, challenges in maintaining pregnancy, and hormonal imbalances. They are particularly beneficial for women experiencing symptoms related to progesterone deficiency, such as irregular menstrual cycles, and are also used in hormone replacement therapy (HRT) for postmenopausal women and to support pregnancy.
What happens to your body when you start taking Progesterone?
When you start taking progesterone, your body may experience changes such as improved sleep, mood stabilization, and reduced menopause or PMS symptoms. Some may also notice water retention, breast tenderness, or mild dizziness as the body adjusts. Effects vary based on dosage and individual response.
Who should not take Progesterone Capsules?
Progesterone Capsules may not be suitable for everyone. Women with a history of blood clots, liver disease, known or suspected breast cancer, or unexplained vaginal bleeding should avoid using progesterone without consulting a healthcare provider. It's crucial to discuss your medical history and any current health issues with your healthcare provider to determine if Progesterone Capsules are safe for you.
How to improve Progesterone naturally?
To naturally improve progesterone, focus on a healthy diet rich in zinc, vitamin B6, and magnesium, manage stress to lower cortisol levels, maintain a healthy weight, and get enough sleep. Herbal supplements like chasteberry (vitex) may also support progesterone production.
