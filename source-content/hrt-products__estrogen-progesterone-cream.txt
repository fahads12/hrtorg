Side effects of our Estrogen and Progesterone Cream
While bio-identical hormone creams are designed to be gentle and mimic the body's natural hormones, some users may experience mild side effects such as skin irritation at the application site, changes in menstrual cycle, or breast tenderness. What are the benefits of combined estrogen and progesterone?
Combined estrogen and progesterone therapy offers several benefits, particularly for women going through menopause or hormone imbalance. It helps relieve menopause symptoms like hot flashes, night sweats, and vaginal dryness. This combination also protects the uterus from the risk of endometrial cancer, which can occur with estrogen alone. Additionally, it supports bone health, reducing the risk of osteoporosis, and may improve mood, sleep, and cognitive function. However, it should be used under medical supervision to balance benefits with potential risks.

Who is the Estrogen + Progesterone Cream designed for?
This cream is specifically designed for women experiencing symptoms of hormonal imbalance, such as mood swings, sleep disturbances, and hot flashes. It is ideal for those navigating menopause, perimenopause, or other conditions affecting hormone levels. Consultation with a healthcare provider is recommended to determine if this cream is right for you.
How should the Estrogen + Progesterone Cream be applied?
For optimal absorption, apply the cream to thin-skinned areas such as the inner arms, neck, chest, or abdomen. Follow the recommended dosage and application instructions provided with the product or as advised by your healthcare provider. It's important to rotate the application sites to promote even hormone absorption.
How long does it take to notice benefits from using the Estrogen + Progesterone Cream?
The time to experience noticeable benefits can vary from person to person. Some may observe improvements in symptoms like mood stabilization and sleep quality within a few weeks, while others may take longer. Consistent use as directed by a healthcare provider is crucial for achieving the best outcomes.
Does estrogen progesterone cream cause weight gain?
Estrogen-progesterone cream may cause weight changes, but it's not a direct cause of weight gain for everyone. Some women experience fluid retention, bloating, or increased appetite, which can lead to mild weight gain. However, balanced hormone levels can also help regulate metabolism and reduce weight-related symptoms of hormonal imbalance. Individual responses vary, so it's best to monitor changes and consult a healthcare provider for guidance.
