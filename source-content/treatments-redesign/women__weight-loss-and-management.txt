To maintain weight loss, focus on consistent exercise, balanced nutrition, monitoring portion sizes, and adopting long-term healthy habits that promote sustainable results. Why is it hard to maintain weight loss? Maintaining weight loss can be challenging due to factors like slowed metabolism, hormonal changes, emotional eating, and environmental influences that make it harder to stick to healthy habits. What is the best program to lose weight and keep it off? The best program combines a balanced diet, regular physical activity, behavioral therapy, and personalized support. Programs that focus on long-term lifestyle changes, rather than quick fixes, are most effective for lasting results. How long do you have to maintain weight loss? Maintaining weight loss is a lifelong commitment, but significant benefits can be seen after consistently maintaining weight for 6 months to a year.
