What is Finasteride (Propecia)?
Finasteride (Propecia) is a medication used to treat male pattern baldness by preventing hair loss and promoting regrowth. It works by blocking DHT, a hormone that shrinks hair follicles. It is also prescribed as Proscar for enlarged prostate (BPH) in men.
How to use Finasteride?

Side Effects associated with Finasteride (Propecia)

How does Finasteride (Propecia) work to prevent hair loss?
Finasteride works by targeting the hormonal cause of hair loss. It inhibits the enzyme 5-alpha-reductase, which is responsible for converting testosterone into dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT is a hormone that contributes to hair follicle shrinkage and hair loss in men. By reducing DHT levels, Finasteride helps to halt hair loss and can promote hair regrowth.
How long does it take to see results from Finasteride?
Results from Finasteride can vary among individuals, but most men start to see noticeable improvements in hair growth and reduced hair loss within three to six months of consistent use. Maximum benefits are typically observed after a year of treatment. It's important to continue taking Finasteride as prescribed to maintain hair regrowth and effectiveness of the treatment.
Who should use Finasteride (Propecia)?
Finasteride is intended for the treatment of male pattern hair loss (androgenetic alopecia) in men only. It is most effective for men experiencing hair thinning or gradual hair loss on the top and front of the scalp. Before starting Finasteride, it's crucial to consult a healthcare provider to ensure it's the right treatment for your specific condition and to discuss any potential health concerns.
Is finasteride safe for long-term use?
Yes, finasteride is generally safe for long-term use, but it may cause side effects like decreased libido or erectile dysfunction in some men. Regular check-ups with a doctor are recommended to monitor any long-term effects.
