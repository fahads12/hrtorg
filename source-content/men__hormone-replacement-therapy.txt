FAQs about hormone replacement therapy for men
What are signs of low testosterone in males?
‍
Common signs include fatigue, reduced libido, erectile dysfunction, loss of muscle mass, increased body fat, mood changes, and difficulty concentrating.
What does hormone replacement therapy do to a man?
‍
Hormone replacement therapy (HRT) helps restore testosterone levels, improving energy, mood, sexual function, muscle strength, and overall well-being.
What are the side effects of hormone replacement therapy in men?
‍
Possible side effects include acne, fluid retention, increased red blood cell count, mood swings, and in some cases, changes in cholesterol levels or prostate health.
What are the signs that you need hormone replacement therapy in men?
‍
Signs include persistent fatigue, low sex drive, depression, reduced muscle mass, and confirmed low testosterone levels through a blood test.
How much does male hormone replacement therapy cost?
‍
Costs vary depending on the type of treatment, provider, and location, but generally range from $100 to $500 per month, including consultations and prescriptions.
