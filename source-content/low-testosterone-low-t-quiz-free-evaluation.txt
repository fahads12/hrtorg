The powerful hormone testosterone, mainly produced in the testicles, drives many physical and mental processes for males even before they’re born.
Testosterone
, necessary for the development of male sex organs, increases throughout childhood and puberty to stimulate the growth of body hair, build bone mass and muscles, deepen the voice, help produce red blood cells, and ramp up sex drive and sperm production for reproductive purposes. Testosterone levels peak at about age 18, however.
When would I need to test my testosterone level?
By the time a man reaches age 30, testosterone levels begin to decrease at a rate of about 1% a year. Many men fear the loss of testosterone as they age and begin to see signs of Low T that significantly impact their lives.
Symptoms of low testosterone include:
Loss of sex drive
Difficulty maintaining an erection
Low level of seminal fluid
Fatigue
Decrease in energy
Depression
Loss of confidence
Irritability
Reduced muscle mass
Fat gain in the midsection
Gynecomastia (male breasts)
Shrinkage of testicles
Loss of hair on scalp
Medical Conditions Associated With Low T
Many serious medical conditions have been linked to low testosterone levels in men. While there may be no direct cause and effect link between testosterone loss and some disease processes, many males diagnosed with diabetes, metabolic syndrome, obesity, and
high blood pressure
, also test positive for low testosterone.
Osteoporosis
, which weakens the bones, is also associated with low testosterone levels in men.
FAQs about low testosterone levels
What happens if you have low testosterone?
Low testosterone can affect many aspects of health, leading to
reduced sex drive, fatigue, muscle loss, mood changes, weight gain, and difficulty concentrating
. It can also impact bone density and overall well-being.
What are symptoms of too low testosterone?
Symptoms of very low testosterone include
low libido, erectile dysfunction, depression, irritability, decreased muscle mass, increased body fat, and low energy levels
.
How do you fix low testosterone levels?
Low testosterone can be treated through
testosterone replacement therapy (TRT)
, as well as
lifestyle changes
like improved diet, regular exercise, weight management, stress reduction, and better sleep.
Can low testosterone go back to normal?
Yes, in some cases low testosterone levels can
return to normal
with lifestyle changes or by treating underlying health issues. In other cases,
TRT may be needed long-term
under medical supervision.
