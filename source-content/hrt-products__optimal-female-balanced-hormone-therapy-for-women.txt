How does the Optimal Female Hormone Therapy Test Kit work?
The Optimal Female Hormone Therapy Test Kit is designed for easy at-home use. Upon receiving the kit, you'll collect a small sample, following the detailed instructions provided. This could be a saliva, blood spot, or urine sample, depending on the hormones being assessed. Then, you send the sample back to our lab using the prepaid shipping label included in the kit. Your results will be analyzed by healthcare professionals, who will then discuss the findings with you during a personalized telehealth consultation.
What are optimal female hormone levels?
Optimal female hormone levels vary by age, menstrual cycle phase, and individual health. Generally, estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone levels should be balanced to support menstrual regularity, mood, energy, and overall health. Optimal levels are typically assessed through blood tests, with specific reference ranges provided by healthcare providers based on your unique circumstances.
Who should consider using the Optimal Female Hormone Therapy Test Kit?
