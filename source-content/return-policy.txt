Return Policy
HRT Return Policy
At HRT, your health and satisfaction are our top priorities. Due to the nature of our products and services, we follow strict guidelines to ensure safety, legal compliance, and transparency.
Prescription Medications and Telehealth Services
Prescription medications, once dispensed,
cannot be returned or refunded
. This includes any custom-compounded products and medications prescribed after a telehealth consultation.
‍
This policy is in accordance with federal regulations and protects the integrity of the medication supply chain.
Telehealth consultation fees are
non-refundable
once the consultation has taken place, regardless of prescription outcome.
Lab Kits and At-Home Test Kits
Unopened and unused at-home lab kits may be eligible for a return within
30 days
of delivery.
Returns must be requested through our support team.
Returned kits must be sealed and unused.
Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.
A refund will be issued once the return is received and inspected.
Supplements and Non-Prescription Products
Unopened supplements and non-prescription wellness products may be returned within
30 days
of delivery.
Products must be in original, sealed packaging.
Return shipping costs apply.
A full refund will be processed once the item is received and inspected.
How to Request a Return
Please contact our support team at
support@hrt.org
with your order number, the product(s) you wish to return, and the reason for the return. Our team will provide return instructions and shipping details.
Note:
We do not accept returns for opened lab kits, used products, or medications. We reserve the right to deny returns that do not meet the above criteria.
