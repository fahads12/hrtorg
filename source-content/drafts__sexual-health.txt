Sex and physical intimacy are an important part of overall health and wellness for most adults.
‍
Sometimes however, due to medications, a physical condition, or mental health issues, sexual health suffers.
As men and women age, and hormone levels decline, loss of libido, erectile dysfunction, or painful intercourse may also make sex less frequent, less pleasurable, or mechanically difficult.
Many times, medical treatments can restore healthy sexual function in both men and women.
43% of women and 31% of men report some degree of sexual dysfunction in their lifetime.
