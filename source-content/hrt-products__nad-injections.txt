What are NAD+ injections, and how do they work?
NAD+ injections contain Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide (NAD+), a vital coenzyme present in all cells. They work by replenishing the body's levels of NAD+, which naturally decline with age. NAD+ supplement injections are typically administered intramuscularly or subcutaneously, depending on your healthcare provider’s instructions.
