What happens when your thyroid is off balance?
When your thyroid is off balance, it can disrupt metabolism and affect energy levels, weight, mood, and overall health. An underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism) can cause fatigue, weight gain, and depression, while an overactive thyroid (hyperthyroidism) may lead to weight loss, anxiety, and rapid heart rate. Proper diagnosis and treatment are essential for restoring balance.
How do I use the thyroid test kit at home?
Using the thyroid test kit at home is simple and convenient. Follow the detailed instructions included in your kit to collect a small blood sample, typically through a finger prick. Once you've collected your sample, place it in the provided return packaging and mail it back to our lab using the prepaid shipping label. Ensure your sample is properly sealed and packaged to prevent any damage or contamination during transit.
How will I receive my test results, and what will they tell me?
You will receive your test results electronically through our secure platform. The results will include detailed readings of your TSH, Free T4, and Free T3 levels, along with an interpretation of what these levels mean. This comprehensive report will help you understand your thyroid function and identify any imbalances that may require further attention or lifestyle adjustments.
What should I do if my test results indicate a thyroid imbalance?
If your test results suggest a thyroid imbalance, it's important to follow up with a healthcare professional. You will have access to a telehealth consultation with one of our healthcare providers, who can discuss your results, answer any questions, and provide guidance on the next steps. This may include recommendations for lifestyle changes, further diagnostic testing, or referral to a specialist if necessary.
