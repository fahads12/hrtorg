Jessica Kikla ARNP, Nurse Practitioner
ARNP, Nurse Practitioner
Jessica is a Dual Certified Adult & Gerontological Nurse Practitioner specializing in Anti-Aging and Hormone Replacement Therapy.
She received her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Florida and soon after moved to Maryland to work at the World-Renowned Shock Trauma Hospital in Baltimore. Here she worked in emergent situations and provided vital care to the critically sick and severely injured. During this time, she completed her Master’s Degree at the University of Maryland, graduating in 2012.
Prior to coming to this practice, she was a Lead Instructor in Shock wave therapy for ED, teaching other physicians proper protocols and techniques. She is also a certified instructor in PRP. She has extensive knowledge in Hormone Optimization and received Peptide Certification through the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine in 2018.
