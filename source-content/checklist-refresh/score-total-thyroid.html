What does this mean?

The thyroid is a small butterfly-shaped gland located in front of the windpipe and responsible for the production of hormones, thyroxine (T4), triiodothyronine (T3), and calcitonin. It produces thyroid hormone (TH) that affects how the body uses energy, regulating temperature, metabolism, and heartbeat.
Based on your thyroid assessment and symptoms, you may be suffering from a thyroid disorder.

What are my next steps?

See a healthcare practitioner to discuss symptoms and possible testing, or order tests on your own and share results with your doctor. Treatment for thyroid disorders may include:
  • Oral medications
  • Radioactive iodine treatment
  • Surgical removal of benign nodules or cancer
  • Surgical removal of the thyroid gland
Natural support may also include a thyroid/adrenal supplement, a diet fortified with sea vegetables, regular exercise, and cutting out refined carbohydrates.
