What is Sildenafil?

How should I take Sildenafil for the best results?
Sildenafil should be taken orally, approximately 30 to 60 minutes before planned sexual activity. It can be taken with or without food, though a high-fat meal may delay its effectiveness. Are there any side effects associated with taking Sildenafil?
While Sildenafil is safe for most men, some may experience side effects. Common side effects include headache, flushing, indigestion, nasal congestion, dizziness, and visual disturbances. How does Sildenafil work to treat erectile dysfunction (ED)?
Sildenafil works by inhibiting an enzyme called phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5), which is responsible for the degradation of cGMP in the penis. By blocking PDE5, Sildenafil increases the levels of cGMP, leading to the relaxation of smooth muscle tissues in the penis and allowing for increased blood flow. This process facilitates the achievement and maintenance of an erection when combined with sexual stimulation.
Is Viagra stronger than generic Sildenafil?
No, Viagra and generic Sildenafil contain the same active ingredient and have the same strength, effectiveness, and safety profile. The primary difference is in branding and price.
Who should not take Sildenafil?
Sildenafil is not suitable for everyone. Individuals taking nitrates for chest pain, those with severe heart or liver problems, or those who have had a recent stroke or heart attack should not take Sildenafil. Additionally, it should not be used by individuals who are allergic to any components of the medication. Always consult with a healthcare provider to determine if Sildenafil is safe for you.
How much does Sildenafil cost?
The cost of Sildenafil varies depending on the dosage, quantity, and pharmacy. Generic versions are typically much more affordable than Viagra.
