Are there any side effects associated with Cialis?
Common side effects of Cialis include headache, indigestion, back pain, muscle aches, flushed skin, and stuffy or runny nose. These side effects usually go away after a few hours. However, Cialis can cause serious side effects in rare cases, such as an erection lasting more than 4 hours, sudden vision loss in one or both eyes, or sudden hearing decrease or loss. If you experience any of these, seek immediate medical attention.

What is the recommended starting dose of Cialis for erectile dysfunction (ED)?
The recommended starting dose of Cialis for most men with erectile dysfunction is 10mg, taken prior to anticipated sexual activity. Depending on the individual's response and tolerance, the dosage may be adjusted to 20mg or reduced to 5mg. It's important to follow your healthcare provider's instructions and not to take more than one dose in 24 hours.
Can Cialis be used for conditions other than erectile dysfunction?
Yes, apart from treating erectile dysfunction, Cialis (Tadalafil) is also approved for the treatment of symptoms associated with benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), such as difficulty in starting the flow of urine, weak stream, and the need to urinate frequently or urgently. It works by relaxing the muscles in the prostate and bladder. Always consult a healthcare provider for an accurate diagnosis and appropriate treatment options.
How long does it take for Cialis to work?
Cialis typically starts working within 30 to 60 minutes of ingestion. It's important to note that sexual stimulation is required to achieve an erection with Cialis. The medication can remain effective for up to 36 hours, allowing for greater flexibility and spontaneity in sexual activities.
Does generic Cialis exist?
