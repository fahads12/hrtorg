What is Anastrozole (Arimidex), and how does it work?
Anastrozole, sold under the brand name Arimidex, is a medication used in the treatment of hormone receptor-positive breast cancer in postmenopausal women. It's classified as an aromatase inhibitor, which works by lowering the body's estrogen levels. Since some breast cancers grow faster in the presence of estrogen, reducing its production can help halt the growth and spread of these estrogen-dependent cancer cells.
‍
How to use Anastrozole (Arimidex)?
Anastrozole (Arimidex) is usually taken as a once-daily oral tablet, with or without food. It’s important to follow your healthcare provider’s dosage instructions closely, typically 1 mg per day. The medication is often prescribed as part of hormone therapy for conditions like estrogen-sensitive cancers or hormone imbalance in men.
‍
Are there any side effects associated with Anastrozole (Arimidex)?
While Anastrozole is effective in treating breast cancer, it can cause side effects in some patients. Common side effects include hot flashes, joint pain or stiffness, weakness, mood changes, and nausea. More serious side effects can occur, such as bone thinning (osteoporosis), increased cholesterol levels, and liver problems. It's crucial to discuss the potential risks and benefits of Anastrozole with your healthcare provider and report any side effects you experience.

Is there a difference between Arimidex and Anastrozole?
Arimidex is the brand name for the drug anastrozole. There is no difference in effectiveness; both contain the same active ingredient. The generic version (anastrozole) is often more affordable while providing the same therapeutic benefits.
Who should take Anastrozole (Arimidex)?
Anastrozole is prescribed to postmenopausal women diagnosed with hormone receptor-positive breast cancer. It can be used as part of adjuvant therapy (treatment given after the primary treatment to increase the chances of a cure) for early-stage breast cancer or for the treatment of advanced stages of the disease. Your healthcare provider can determine if Anastrozole is the right treatment for you based on your specific condition and medical history.
How is Anastrozole (Arimidex) taken?
Anastrozole is taken orally, usually once a day, with or without food. It's important to take it at the same time every day, following the dosage and duration as prescribed by your healthcare provider. Consistency in taking your medication as directed is key to the effectiveness of the treatment.
How much does anastrozole cost?
The cost of anastrozole varies depending on location, insurance, and pharmacy, but generic versions typically range from $50 to $130 per month. Brand-name Arimidex may cost significantly more without insurance coverage.
