How to Know if You’re in Perimenopause
Perimenopause is typically identified by irregular menstrual cycles combined with new physical or emotional changes. Fluctuations in estrogen and progesterone may cause periods to become lighter, heavier, or less predictable. If you’re also noticing symptoms like hot flashes, sleep disturbances, or mood swings, it could be a sign that perimenopause has begun. While every woman’s experience is unique, keeping track of your symptoms is an important first step.
Common and Unusual Signs of Perimenopause
Symptoms associated with Perimenopause are as individual as the women suffering from them. For the Self-Assessment below, we have narrowed the selection of symptoms to those most commonly reported. The most common signs of perimenopause include hot flashes, night sweats, irregular periods, vaginal dryness, and changes in mood or energy. However, there are also less obvious symptoms that women sometimes overlook, such as brain fog, anxiety, heart palpitations, or joint pain. Because these changes can mimic other conditions, it’s important not to dismiss them. Recognizing both the expected and unusual signs of perimenopause can help you seek support sooner.

The results of your quiz will help you understand whether your symptoms align with the typical stages of perimenopause. FAQs About Testing for Perimenopause
How can I test myself for perimenopause?
‍
You can start by tracking your symptoms with an online self-assessment quiz. Hormone blood tests ordered by a doctor can confirm perimenopause by checking estrogen, progesterone, and FSH levels.
Can you test for perimenopause at home?
‍
Yes, at-home hormone test kits are available. They typically measure key hormones like FSH and estrogen through saliva, urine, or finger-prick blood samples.
What can be mistaken for perimenopause?
‍
Thyroid disorders, stress, depression, and certain vitamin deficiencies can produce symptoms that mimic perimenopause. This is why professional testing is important for an accurate diagnosis.
What are the first signs of perimenopause?
‍
The earliest signs often include irregular periods, hot flashes, sleep problems, and mood swings. Some women also notice changes in libido and vaginal dryness.
Which stage of perimenopause am I in?
‍
Perimenopause is divided into early and late stages. In the early stage, cycles become irregular but still occur, while in the late stage, periods are more widely spaced until they stop altogether at menopause.
