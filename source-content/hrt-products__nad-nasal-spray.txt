What is NAD Nasal Spray?
NAD nasal spray is a fast-acting delivery method for Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide, a coenzyme essential to energy production and cellular repair. This innovative formulation allows for rapid absorption through the nasal passages, supporting brain function, metabolism, and overall vitality. It’s often used to combat fatigue, enhance mental clarity, and promote healthy aging by boosting mitochondrial function and aiding in DNA repair.
How to Use the NAD Plus Nose Spray
To use the NAD Plus nasal spray, gently insert the nozzle into one nostril while blocking the other, then press down to administer the recommended dose. Repeat in the other nostril if instructed. The spray is typically used once or twice daily, depending on your treatment plan. It’s best taken at consistent times each day, and users are advised not to blow their nose immediately after use. Always follow the instructions provided by your healthcare provider for optimal absorption and effectiveness.
Side effects of NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide)
NAD+ is generally well-tolerated, especially when used as a nasal spray. However, some users may experience mild side effects such as nasal irritation, headaches, dizziness, or slight congestion. These symptoms are usually temporary. Rarely, more noticeable effects like fatigue or digestive upset may occur as your body adjusts to increased cellular activity. It’s important to follow the recommended dosage and consult your provider if side effects persist or worsen.

What does NAD do for your body?
NAD (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) plays a vital role in cellular energy production, DNA repair, and brain function. It supports healthy aging, enhances metabolism, and helps maintain optimal cellular performance.
Who should avoid NAD?
Individuals who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have certain medical conditions such as active cancer or liver disease should consult a healthcare provider before using NAD supplements or sprays.
