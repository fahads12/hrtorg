How to Use our Semaglutide + BioBoost Injections at home
To use Semaglutide + BioBoost injections at home, start by washing your hands and cleaning the vial’s rubber top with an alcohol swab. Draw the prescribed dose into a sterile syringe, making sure to remove any air bubbles. Choose an injection site, such as the abdomen or thigh, and clean it with an alcohol swab. Insert the needle at a 90-degree angle, inject the solution slowly, then dispose of the syringe in a proper sharps container. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions for dosage and frequency.
Side effects of Semaglutide + BioBoost Injections
Common side effects of Semaglutide can include gastrointestinal issues such as nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, stomach pain, and loss of appetite. These side effects are generally mild to moderate and diminish over time. BioBoost injections are usually well-tolerated, but as with any injection, there may be some localized reaction at the injection site. Always discuss potential side effects and any concerns with your healthcare provider.

How do Semaglutide + BioBoost injections work for weight loss?
