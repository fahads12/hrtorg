Most Popular Products
Stress B-Complex
The Stress B-Complex by Thorne promotes adrenal support and stress management. This particular formula is superior to other products because it contains some of the B vitamins in activated form, ensuring better absorption into the body. This supplement consists of six different forms of the B vitamin and two other water soluble compounds: vitamin B1, vitamin B2, vitamin B3, vitamin B5, vitamin B6, vitamin B12, folate, and biotin. These compounds work together to promote various biochemical functions. This particular formula also contains an elevated level of the vitamin B5 to support healthy adrenal activity, a known factor in managing stress.
Everyone has something to gain from supplementing with Stress-B Complex, but it is especially useful if you suffer from compromised liver function, poorly functioning enzymes, digestive disturbances, advanced age, or other factors that prohibit your body from properly converting non-active forms of vitamins to active forms.
Vitamin D-5,000
Most people know that your body manufactures Vitamin D when your skin is exposed to the sun; however, due to seasonal changes, people residing in more Northern areas and lifestyles that no longer encourage outdoor activity, individuals are suffering from low Vitamin D levels. Vitamin D is essential for maintaining bone and muscle health and for avoiding bone-related disease. Unfortunately, a problem you may face when looking for a Vitamin D supplement is the use of ingredients with the potential to be detrimental; these include lactose, and the preservatives BHT, BHA, sodium benzoate, and sorbic acid. With Thorne products, you can always be sure that the components used are 100% pure and natural. Their Vitamin D-5,000 formula contains none of the ingredients listed above, but instead, starts with a raw, undiluted form of Vitamin D and the final mixture is made using natural plant powders and other natural components like leucine and silicon dioxide.
This supplement is NSF Certified for Sport and is intended for anyone looking to maintain optimal levels of Vitamin D. Thorne also offers Vitamin D-2,500 and Vitamin D-10,000 so you can find the supplement level best suited for your needs.
