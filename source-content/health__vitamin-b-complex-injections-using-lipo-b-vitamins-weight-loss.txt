Vitamin B Complex for Health and Wellness
Vitamin B complex is comprised of 8 B vitamins including B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B9, and B12. Vitamin B Complex is essential for the human body for a number of reasons. Each of the B vitamins works independently to convert food into energy, promote healthy skin and hair, prevent migraines, and aid in the prevention of memory loss. Vitamin B12 helps the body produce new, healthy cells, protects the nerves, and aids in heart health by lowering levels of homocysteine, a protein that puts individuals at risk for heart attack and stroke. When combined with lipotropic compounds like Methionine, Inositol, and Choline, as in
B12 + MIC injections
, it can also support fat metabolism, liver function, and energy production, making it a popular option in weight management programs.
Vitamin B12 assists in the metabolism of fats, proteins and carbohydrates.
It helps to boost energy, and to increase levels of activity. While B12 is found naturally in foods like, fish, red meat, liver, fortified cereals and dairy products, many of us are deficient in this nutrient because of stress and poor diet.[5]

Even dangerous visceral fat that wraps around the heart, liver, kidneys, and pancreas can be eliminated through diet, exercise, and B complex injections. By harnessing the power of the body’s natural fat burning capabilities with Lipo B, individuals begin to build lean muscle mass, sculpt and tone problem areas, and convert calories into energy, efficiently and effectively.

Vitamin B-complex contains all of the water-soluble essential vitamins (with the exception of vitamin C). These include, thiamine (vitamin B1), riboflavin (vitamin B2), niacin (vitamin B3), pantothenic acid (vitamin B5), pyridoxine (vitamin B6), biotin, folic acid and the
cobalamins, such as vitamin B12 injections
.[4] Vitamin B is actually comprised of several vitamins, individually designated by number. Each of these B vitamins serves a unique purpose and supports a specific function within the body. Vitamins B1, B2, B3, and biotin, assist in energy production. Vitamin B6 aids in the metabolism of amino acids, and vitamin B12 and folic acid facilitate cell division.[7]

Inositol
Choline
Methionine
L-Carnitine

Lipotropic compounds accelerate the breakdown of fat in the metabolic process and decrease fatty liver deposits. The liver plays a key role in controlling body weight. If it is slow to process fats, then overall weight loss will be sluggish as well. Lipotropic compounds contained in Lipo B injections include Choline, Methionine, Inositol, L-Carnitine and Chromium. Each of these aids the liver in the breakdown of fats within the body,
accelerating weight loss and creating energy
. There are some fundamental differences in each of the compounds, however.
Methionine—This essential amino acid contains sulfur, helps eliminate excess fat in the liver, restores energy from fatigue and helps promote growth of blood vessels in the body. It also reduces the release of histamines, and may also aid individuals with allergies.
Inositol— Produced by the human body from glucose, this natural compound aids in the breakdown of fat, and helps with control over concentration. It may also have therapeutic effects in the treatment of depression, panic disorder and obsessive-compulsive disorder, as well as control appetite.[3]
Choline—This macronutrient aids in metabolic function, liver function, brain development, nerve function, the movement of muscles, and the maintenance of optimal energy levels.
L-Carnitine—Created in the liver and kidneys, this amino acid aids in the conversion of fat into energy. In eukaryotic cells, it is specifically required to transport fatty acids.[1]
Chromium— This mineral, needed in small amounts by the body, mobilizes blood sugar into cells, converting it to energy. It metabolizes fats, proteins, and carbohydrates as well.[2]
