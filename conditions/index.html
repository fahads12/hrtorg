For Women

Conditions tied to the female endocrine shift.

Condition

Perimenopause

The 4–10 year hormonal transition before menopause. Fluctuating estrogen and dropping progesterone drive sleep, mood, and cycle changes.

See the full guide
Condition

Menopause

12 months without a period marks menopause. Vasomotor, genitourinary, cognitive, and bone impacts resolve with evidence-based HRT.

See the full guide
Condition

PCOS / PMDD

Polycystic ovary syndrome and premenstrual dysphoric disorder. Insulin resistance, androgen excess, and cycle dysregulation managed with a layered protocol.

See the full guide
Condition

Endometriosis

Ectopic endometrial-like tissue causing pain, heavy periods, and fertility impacts. Hormonal suppression, surgical referral, and symptom-targeted care.

See the full guide
Condition

Fibroids

Uterine leiomyomas — benign smooth-muscle tumors. Heavy bleeding, bulk symptoms, and HRT compatibility considerations.

See the full guide
Condition

PMS

Cyclic physical and emotional symptoms in the luteal phase. Progesterone, SSRI, and lifestyle protocols by symptom pattern.

See the full guide
Condition

UTI, BV & Yeast

Genitourinary infections that cluster in perimenopause and menopause as the vaginal microbiome shifts. Vaginal estrogen is often the long-term fix.

See the full guide
For Men

Conditions tied to the male endocrine shift.

Condition

Low Testosterone

Clinical hypogonadism and symptomatic low T. Workup distinguishes primary vs. secondary; treatment with TRT or TRT-safe fertility protocols.

See the full guide
Condition

Andropause

The male midlife endocrine shift. Testosterone decline interacts with thyroid, metabolic, and cognitive aging.

See the full guide
Everyone

Conditions that affect both audiences.

Condition

Hypothyroidism

Under-active thyroid. Fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, hair loss, cognitive slowing. Levothyroxine and often added T3 restore function.

See the full guide
Condition

Hashimoto's

Autoimmune thyroiditis — the most common cause of hypothyroidism. Antibody-positive dysfunction, often with cycling symptoms.

See the full guide
Condition

Hyperthyroidism

Over-active thyroid. Anxiety, weight loss, palpitations, heat intolerance. Workup for Graves, toxic nodule, or thyroiditis.

See the full guide
Condition

Insulin Resistance

Cells stop responding to insulin. Weight gain, metabolic signaling disruption, early marker for type 2 diabetes.

See the full guide
Condition

Prediabetes

Fasting glucose 100–125 or HbA1c 5.7–6.4. Reversible with weight loss, exercise, and sometimes metformin or GLP-1 therapy.

See the full guide
Condition

Metabolic Syndrome

Cluster of insulin resistance, abdominal obesity, dyslipidemia, and hypertension. Hormone status and lifestyle both drive it.

See the full guide
Condition

Adrenal / HPA dysregulation

Not true adrenal failure. HPA-axis rhythm disruption causing morning fatigue, mid-day crashes, and sleep problems. Treated with lifestyle, lab-guided support, and sometimes replacement.

See the full guide
Condition

Osteoporosis

Low bone mineral density and fracture risk. HRT and TRT protect bone; also treated with weight-bearing exercise, Vit D, and specific pharmacotherapy.

See the full guide
