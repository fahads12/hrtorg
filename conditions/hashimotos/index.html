Hashimoto's Thyroiditis

An autoimmune attack on the thyroid gland and the most common cause of hypothyroidism in the U.S.

Overview

Hashimoto's involves T-cell mediated destruction of thyroid tissue. TPO antibodies are usually elevated years before TSH becomes abnormal. Treatment addresses the autoimmune component (gut health, nutrient support, selenium) alongside thyroid replacement when indicated.
